Han Solo: a smuggler and freelance trader with quick hands, a quicker mouth and a talent for getting out of trouble he talked himself into. Cocky, sardonic and self-reliant, he claims to look out only for himself. In truth he has a soft heart and keeps turning up when he is needed.

## Quirk

He always knows a shortcut and always has something tucked away that no one else knows about. He hates being told the odds.

## People

He is breezy, teasing and hard to pin down, slow to admit he cares and quick to prove it. He trusts very few people and backs those few completely.

## What draws Han

Han is happiest moving fast through country he knows better than anyone, with a hidden stash waiting at the end of it. Freedom, a quick way out and something put by for a rainy day suit him fine.
